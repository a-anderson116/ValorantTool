Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VCT Scout ("we," "us," or "the Service") — a non-commercial, educational match-intelligence and scouting tool for collegiate Valorant programs — collects, uses, and protects information. By using the Service you agree to the practices described here.

VCT Scout is an independent project. It is not endorsed by Riot Games and does not reflect the views or opinions of Riot Games or anyone officially involved in producing or managing Riot Games properties, including VALORANT.

01Information We Collect

We aim to collect the minimum information needed to operate the Service:

  • Riot account data (via RSO): If you sign in with Riot Sign On (RSO), we receive your Riot account identifier (PUUID), game name, and tagline, along with an access token used solely to authenticate your session and retrieve permitted match data.
  • Game and match data: Publicly available VALORANT match, player, and statistics data retrieved through the Riot Games API and/or third-party community APIs, used to display scoreboards, scouting reports, and analytics.
  • Usage data: Basic technical information such as browser type, pages viewed, and error logs, used to keep the Service running and secure.

We do not collect payment information, government identifiers, or passwords. Authentication is handled entirely by Riot; we never see your Riot password.

02How We Use Information

  • To authenticate you and maintain your signed-in session.
  • To retrieve and display match, player, and team statistics for scouting and analysis.
  • To generate scouting reports and analytics for coaches and analysts at collegiate programs.
  • To operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Service.

03Legal Basis & Purpose Limitation

VCT Scout is provided for non-commercial, educational purposes supporting collegiate esports programs. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling unrelated to gameplay analytics.

04Player Opt-In & Consent

Opt-in required. VCT Scout only displays a player's VALORANT stats and gameplay data after that player has signed in and opted in through Riot Sign On (RSO). Players who have not opted in will not have their data made available to other players through this website, its applications, or any game overlay.

By signing in with RSO and using the Service, you consent to VCT Scout retrieving and displaying your VALORANT match and statistics data for scouting and analytics purposes. You may withdraw this consent at any time by logging out and revoking VCT Scout's access in your Riot account settings, after which your data will no longer be displayed and any cached data will be removed.

05Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Information may be processed by the infrastructure providers that host the Service (for example, Vercel and Railway) and by data providers whose APIs we call (Riot Games and community APIs such as HenrikDev). These providers process data only as needed to deliver the Service and under their own privacy terms.

06Data Retention

Session tokens are short-lived and are cleared when you log out or when they expire. Cached match and statistics data is retained only as long as needed to provide analytics features and may be refreshed or deleted at any time.

07Your Choices

  • You can log out at any time to end your session and clear your local session data.
  • You may revoke VCT Scout's access to your Riot account through your Riot account settings.
  • You may request deletion of any stored data associated with your account by contacting us.

08Security

We use industry-standard measures — including HTTPS transport encryption and restricted server-side handling of API credentials — to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

09Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for coaches, analysts, and players at accredited post-secondary institutions and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

10Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
